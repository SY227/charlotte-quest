CHARLOTTE'S QUEST — QUICK START

1. Open Terminal.
2. Go to this folder:

   cd /path/to/charlotte-quest

3. Add the Gemini key:

   bash setup-key.sh

4. Start:

   npm start

5. On the iPad, open the "iPad:" address printed in Terminal.

The built-in Multiplication & Array Adventure works even without the key.
The key is required only to analyze new homework photos with Gemini.
